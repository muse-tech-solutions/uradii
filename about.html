About Uradii

Rooted in Laikipia, working for the long haul

Who We Are

A community-based organization built by residents, for residents

Uradii Initiative CBO was formed by residents of Nanyuki and the wider Laikipia County to address challenges we see and live with every day — from youth livelihoods to the safety of the roads our children walk to school.

We work alongside local government, schools, transport operators and grassroots groups, believing lasting change is built together, not delivered from outside.

Core Values

What guides our work

Our Vision

A Laikipia where every community member has the opportunity, safety and support to thrive.

Our Mission

To empower communities and young people through grassroots programmes in livelihoods, youth development and road safety.

Community First

Every programme starts with what residents tell us they need.

Integrity

We are transparent about how we work and what we achieve.

Collaboration

Government, schools and local groups working toward shared goals.

Accountability

We measure our impact and answer to the community we serve.

Our History

How we got here

Founding

Uradii Initiative CBO is formed

A group of residents in Laikipia County come together around a shared concern for community wellbeing and safety.

General Assembly

Members convene to set direction

The General Assembly confirms our three focus areas — community empowerment, youth and road safety — and lays the groundwork for social media and public outreach.

2026

Schools Road Safety Campaign launches

Our flagship pilot begins in St. Moses Primary and Nanyuki Primary, with a roadmap to reach five more schools.
